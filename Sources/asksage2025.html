Built for All Missions

Ask Sage is a secure, FedRAMP® High and DoW IL5, IL6 and Top Secret-authorized generative AI platform, designed to empower the Defense Industrial Base (DIB) with cutting-edge technology to drive innovation, enhance collaboration, ensure security compliance, and optimize operational efficiency. From business development and capture to acquisition and execution, Ask Sage accelerates decision-making, strengthens wartime readiness, and enables seamless integration across mission-critical operations. Whether supporting defense contractors, suppliers, or partners, Ask Sage is built to deliver results across the entire defense ecosystem.

Chief Digital and AI Office (CDAO)

The CDAO provides unlimited Ask Sage access to all Combatant Commands, the Joint Staff, and the Office of the Secretary of War at IL5 and IL6. These teams use Ask Sage to streamline acquisition workflows, generating RFPs, acquisition strategies, and related documents while embedding context, enforcing format and tone, and saving teams hours of rewriting.

  • Embedded Structure: Produces compliant, formatted acquisition documents
  • Context-Aware: Adapts to agency language and evolving guidance
  • Time-Saving: Reduces manual review cycles and duplicated effort
  • Team-Proof: Delivers consistency across changing personnel and projects

Army Enterprise LLM Workspace

The U.S. Army uses Ask Sage to power its enterprise LLM workspace, deployed across IL5 environments and accessible to soldiers, civilians, and contractors through the cArmy Cloud. Accredited for Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), it’s available through CAC login without installation, modernizing workflows and accelerating mission decision-making.

  • Secure & Accessible: CUI-accredited and hosted in the cArmy Cloud
  • Easy to Adopt: SaaS-based, no install required, CAC-authenticated
  • Proven Impact: Reclassified 300,000 personnel descriptions in one week, saving 50,000+ hours
  • Flexible Licensing: 30-day free trial with token-based procurement post-trial

Mission-Ready Solutions for the Defense Ecosystem

Accelerate Authorization with ATO Automation

Ask Sage’s ATO In a Box is tailored to meet the unique needs of the Defense Industrial Base (DIB), enabling defense teams to streamline compliance documentation processes. By automating the generation of System Security Plans (SSPs), policies, and NIST controls, the platform empowers organizations to achieve faster and more efficient ATO approvals. In a deployment for a major combatant command, Ask Sage’s ATO In a Box was instrumental in drafting and refining documentation for multiple software products, significantly reducing manual effort and expediting the path to authorization.

  • Time Savings: Reduced over 528 hours of manual work, allowing defense teams to allocate resources to mission-critical operations
  • Cost Avoidance: Achieved a 90% reduction in document production costs, saving over $84,000 compared to traditional methods
  • Scalability: Processed more than 669,000 tokens across a wide range of document types, ensuring consistent and repeatable outputs for large-scale operations
  • Improved Compliance: Leveraged embedded templates and AI-driven quality assurance to maintain alignment with FedRAMP®, CMMC, CMMI, and DoW (c)ATO/RMF requirements

Edge Computing for Mission-Critical Operations

Ask Sage is purpose-built to operate in disconnected, degraded, intermittent, and limited (DDIL) environments, delivering Generative AI capabilities directly to the tactical edge. Whether deployed at forward operating bases, maritime command centers, or other critical defense locations, the platform empowers defense teams to securely exploit data and perform real-time analysis without reliance on cloud connectivity.

  • Mission-Ready Design: Operates seamlessly in air-gapped or classified environments with SWaP-optimized (Size, Weight, and Power) hardware and zero trust security, ensuring secure and reliable performance in austere and high-risk conditions
  • On-Site Data Processing: Enables real-time analysis of sensitive datasets locally, eliminating the risks and delays associated with cloud-based data transfers, ensuring immediate access to actionable intelligence
  • Rapid Deployment: Deployable within minutes, allowing defense teams and commanders to adapt quickly and make informed decisions in dynamic and rapidly changing operational scenarios
  • Customizable Model Support: Supports both open-source and proprietary Large Language Models (LLMs), tailored to mission-specific requirements and optimized for available computational resources

Smarter Business Development, Enhanced Acquisition

Ask Sage empowers teams to produce accurate, compliant, and defensible acquisition documents with unprecedented speed and efficiency. From Statements of Work (SOWs) to Requests for Information (RFIs) and Requests for Proposals (RFPs), the platform leverages built-in templates and existing language while using generative AI to identify gaps, inconsistencies, and areas for improvement in real time.

  • Compliance Confidence: Ensures alignment with FAR, DFARS, and ATO requirements, reducing the risk of protests, delays, or non-compliance issues
  • Speed to First Draft: Generates high-quality draft acquisition documents in minutes, enabling contracting teams to focus on strategic decision-making rather than manual drafting
  • Collaborative Reviews: Facilitates real-time edits and discussions among contracting officers, legal advisors, and program managers, streamlining the review and approval process
  • Insights & Benchmarking: Provides historical trends, vendor performance data, and pricing benchmarks to inform evaluation criteria and enhance decision-making

Code Modernization, Even in Air-Gapped Environments

Ask Sage supports modernization efforts for legacy systems like COBOL and IDEAL, even within classified environments with no internet connectivity. The platform helped an Air Force team migrate legacy code to a new platform, improve documentation, reduce errors, and save time.

  • Time Compression: Reduced code migration timelines from months to weeks, accelerating delivery of new capabilities
  • Cost Efficiency: Automated analysis and refactoring reduced need for manual intervention and legacy expertise
  • Improved Architecture: Rearchitected systems to align with modern standards and make future maintenance easier
  • Human-in-the-Loop Assurance: Ensured mission integrity with structured validation checkpoints by SMEs
